# Espresso machine maintenance

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- The lever machine needs a backflush every Sunday and a full gasket service yearly.
- Water hardness is the enemy: the inline softener cartridge gets replaced every three months.
- Grinder burrs were swapped in March; the new set needs a coarser starting point than the old ones.
- Descaling is NOT needed with the softener in place — skipping it is intentional, not neglect.

### Relationships
- (none)

### Assessment
The Sunday backflush habit has held for a year and the shots are consistent.
Buy softener cartridges two at a time; the supplier's shipping is slow.
