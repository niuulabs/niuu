# Garden irrigation

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Drip irrigation on two zones: vegetable beds (morning, 20 minutes) and the greenhouse (morning and evening, 10 minutes each).
- The controller skips watering when the rain sensor has tripped in the previous 12 hours.
- Winterisation means draining both lines before the first frost — the greenhouse line cracked in 2024 because this was missed.
- Soaker hose pressure must stay below 1.5 bar or the fittings pop.
