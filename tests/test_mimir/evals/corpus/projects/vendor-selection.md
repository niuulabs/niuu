# Flight battery vendor selection

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Nordvolt was selected as the flight battery supplier over Celtix Energy and PolarCell in January 2026.
- Decision criteria: energy density (420 Wh/kg), cold-weather discharge performance, and Scandinavian logistics.
- PolarCell scored better on price but failed the minus-twenty-degree discharge test.
- Contract includes firmware-update SLAs added after the February bug.

### Relationships
- [[nordvolt]] — winning vendor.
- [[bjorn-eriksen]] — ran the evaluation for the Helios fleet.

### Assessment
Right call on the cells, incomplete diligence on firmware quality. The SLA
amendment closes the gap on paper; the bench-soak rule closes it in practice.
