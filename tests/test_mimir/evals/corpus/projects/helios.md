# Helios

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Helios is the autonomous drone programme inspecting solar farms and wind turbines.
- Led by Bjorn Eriksen; the largest revenue line at Asgard Robotics.
- Fleet of 18 drones across four customer sites; flight batteries supplied by Nordvolt.
- Launch slipped two weeks in February 2026 because of a battery-management firmware bug.
- Passed 1,000 autonomous inspection flights in May 2026.

### Relationships
- [[bjorn-eriksen]] — programme lead.
- [[nordvolt]] — flight battery supplier.
- [[asgard-robotics]] — flagship programme.

### Assessment
Helios is the commercial bet that has to land. Unit economics turned positive
in April once per-flight battery swaps dropped below two; reliability, not
demand, is the constraint.
