# Orchard

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Orchard is the fruit-picking robot pilot: a wheeled platform with a soft-grip arm for apple harvesting.
- Funded as an experiment with its own budget line, separate from Helios.
- Runs inference on-board using the edge hardware Rune benchmarked; no cloud round-trips during picking.
- One pilot orchard in Hardanger; harvest trial scheduled for September 2026.

### Relationships
- [[asgard-robotics]] — funded experiment.
- [[rune-lindqvist]] — edge inference hardware comes from his benchmarks.

### Assessment
Technically promising, commercially unproven. The September harvest trial is
the go/no-go gate: pick rate above 70% of a human crew keeps it funded.
