# Battery technology survey

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Flight time is the binding constraint on inspection drone economics: every extra minute aloft is one fewer battery swap per shift.
- Current lithium-polymer packs deliver about 38 minutes of inspection flight in summer, 29 in winter.
- Solid-state cells promise 50% better energy density but no vendor ships flight-rated packs before 2028.
- Cold-weather performance varies more between vendors than headline energy density does — this drove the Nordvolt selection.

### Relationships
- [[nordvolt]] — current supplier; their cold-weather numbers anchor the comparison.

### Assessment
Nothing on the market changes the calculus before 2028; the near-term wins
are operational (warm storage between flights) rather than chemistry.
