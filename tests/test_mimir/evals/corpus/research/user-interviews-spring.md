# Spring 2026 operator interviews

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Twelve drone operators interviewed across three wind-farm sites during April 2026.
- Top finding: operators triage alerts by glancing, not reading — they want density and colour-stable severity coding, not cards and whitespace.
- Second finding: keyboard control matters because operators wear gloves half the year; mouse precision drops badly in cold.
- Third finding: trust in automation rises when the dashboard shows why an alert fired, not just what fired.
- Findings feed directly into the Midgard dashboard brief.

### Relationships
- [[sigrid-dahl]] — ran the interviews.
- [[midgard-dashboard]] — primary consumer of the findings.

### Assessment
Unusually consistent signal for a qualitative round — all three findings
appeared at every site. The glove/keyboard finding is the kind of thing no
amount of analytics would have surfaced.
