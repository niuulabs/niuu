# Asgard Robotics

## Compiled Truth

### Key Facts
- Industrial robotics company building autonomous inspection drones and agricultural robots.
- Two active programmes: Helios (solar and wind farm inspection drones) and Orchard (fruit-picking robot pilot).
- Roughly 40 employees; engineering is the largest function.
- Infrastructure runs on Valhalla Cloud; batteries are sourced from Nordvolt.
